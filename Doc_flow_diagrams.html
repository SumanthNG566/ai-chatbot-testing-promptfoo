The flow: what happens, in order

Read it top to bottom. The loop in the middle runs once for each of the 7 learner messages. Every test ends in one of three colours.

PASS · answered and followed every rule FAIL · answered but broke a rule ERROR · no answer came back

  1. Start
    You press Enter.npx fetches Promptfoo the first time and reuses it after that.
  2. Read
    Promptfoo reads your four files.The config says which AI and which 7 tests, prompt.txt holds the bot's 8 rules, courses.txt holds the course list, and .env holds the key. Nothing is sent anywhere yet.
  3. Build
    It fills in the blanks.{{courses}} in prompt.txt gets the whole course list, and {{message}} gets this test's learner message, for example "What is the fee for the Selenium course?". That's exactly what the real chatbot server would send.
  4. Ask
    It asks Gemini.This is the only step that uses the internet. If Gemini can't answer, that test becomes ERROR.
  5. Grade
    It ticks the checklist.Every check must say yes for PASS. One no means FAIL. For tests 4 to 7, one check is an llm-rubric: Promptfoo asks Gemini a second time to judge the reply, like a second teacher.
  6. Repeat
    It loops until all 7 messages are done.Promptfoo asks up to 4 at a time, like 4 checkout lanes in a shop, so the whole run takes about 20 seconds.
  7. Report
    It prints the summary and saves the results.The saved copy is what the browser viewer shows later.

Diagram 2 · handshake

The handshake: who talks to whom

A handshake is the back-and-forth between two programs. Promptfoo rings Gemini's doorbell, shows its library card (your API key) and asks the question. Gemini either answers or says why it won't. Solid arrows are requests; dashed arrows are replies.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  actor You
  participant T as Terminal
  participant P as Promptfoo
  participant F as Your files
  participant G as Gemini (Google)
  participant D as Saved results
  participant B as Browser
  You->>T: npx.cmd promptfoo eval -c learner_bot_eval/promptfooconfig.yaml
  T->>P: Start Promptfoo
  P->>F: Read the config, prompt.txt, courses.txt and .env
  F-->>P: Tests, rules, course list and API key
  loop Once for each of the 7 learner messages
    P->>G: Here is my key and the full prompt
    alt Key OK and model free
      G-->>P: The reply, for example Rs. 18,000
      P->>P: Check the reply against the rules
      opt Tests 4 to 7 use llm-rubric
        P->>G: Second teacher - does this reply follow the rule?
        G-->>P: Yes or no, with a reason
      end
    else Key wrong
      G-->>P: Error 400 or 403
    else Model retired or busy
      G-->>P: Error 404 or 503
    end
  end
  P->>D: Save the results
  P-->>T: Table plus passed, failed, errors
  T-->>You: You read the summary
  You->>T: npx.cmd promptfoo view
  T->>P: Start the viewer on port 15500
  P->>D: Load the saved results
  You->>B: Open localhost:15500
  B->>P: Ask for the results page
  P-->>B: The PASS / FAIL table
  1. 1 – 4
    Getting readyEverything stays on your PC. Promptfoo only reads your files.
  2. 5 – 11
    The handshake with GeminiStep 5 is the knock on the door. Step 6 is a normal answer, and step 7 is Promptfoo grading it. Steps 8 and 9 are the second teacher, used only by tests 4 to 7. Steps 10 and 11 are the two ways the door stays shut: a wrong key, or a model that is retired or too busy.
  3. 12 – 14
    ReportingResults are saved, then printed in the terminal.
  4. 15 – 20
    Viewing in the browserA second, separate command. The viewer is a tiny website on your own PC, so it only works while that terminal stays open. Otherwise the browser says "This site can't be reached".

Reading the ending

Three possible results

ResultWhat happenedSetup working?What to do
PASSGemini answered and every check said yesYesNothing. You're done.
FAILGemini answered, but at least one check said noYes. The test caught a bad answer, or the data changed.Click the cell in the viewer to see which check failed and why
ERRORNo answer came back from GeminiNoCheck the key in .env, wait if Google is busy, or keep gemini-3.5-flash-lite

For a tester, FAIL is the system working. ERROR is the one to fix.
